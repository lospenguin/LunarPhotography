Hey, I'm Brianna.

I'm a photographer based out of the Lehigh Valley in Pennsylvania. I have a studio in Allentown! While you may be confused at first, I do have two names; Brianna Sambrano Photography & Lunar Photography. Lunar Photography is strictly for my Boudoir ladies as it means so much to me.

I've wanted to be a photographer since at least middle school. Growing up, I found out that was the same dream my dad had. Unfortunately, only one of us has been able to follow their dreams, and for that I am so lucky.

My favorite thing to do during my sessions is just to laugh and have fun. I love having fun. You can almost always find me with my hair up in a messy bun and a cup of coffee in my hand.
